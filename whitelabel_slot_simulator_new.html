Rules & notes
  • Slot 1 is always Holidu.
  • Slot 2 is always the market anchor where one exists: Ferienwohnungen (DE, AT), Gîtes (FR). Free in all other markets.
  • Differentiator domains are excluded on broad/phrase — they would otherwise serve on generic d_no_differentiator queries that cannot be controlled at campaign level.
  • Region is a hard gate: a destination domain cannot activate unless its region appears in the keyword.
  • A destination domain with a product tag (e.g. fincamallorca.de = p_finca + Mallorca) scores at priority 3 only when both product AND region are in the keyword.
⚠️ Limitations & data sources
  • Region matching covers only major regions (Mallorca, Spain, Trentino/Südtirol). Sub-regions and cities are not mapped — e.g. "Barcelona" will not trigger ferienwohnungen-spanien.de even though it falls within Spain.
